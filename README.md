# TripCraft : Smart Travel Planner

**Live Demo:** **[View Project](https://lorraine14.github.io/TravelPlanner/)**

---

## Overview

Planning a trip should be exciting, yet the planning process often feels overwhelming. While planning my own trips, I found myself constantly switching between travel blogs, notes, spreadsheets and booking websites just to keep everything organized.

I built **TripCraft** to solve that problem by bringing the entire planning experience into one place. Instead of focusing on booking, TripCraft helps travelers find destinations, organize every aspect of their trip, and feel fully prepared before continuing to trusted booking platforms.

---

## The Problem

Planning a trip usually means managing information across multiple apps and websites.

* Destination seeking is separated from trip planning.
* Budgets, itineraries, and packing lists are often stored in different places.
* Important travel preparations can easily be overlooked.
* Organizing everything becomes more time consuming as the trip grows.

---

## The Solution

TripCraft provides a single workspace where travelers can plan every stage of their journey. From discovering destinations and building itineraries to tracking budgets, packing lists, and travel preparation, everything is organized in one place before users continue to external booking services.
